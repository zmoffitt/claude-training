These are workflow choices. Available tools, account access, and the selected environment determine what a session can actually read or do. A confident answer is not evidence that a repository was inspected. [Official quickstart](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/quickstart).

## One incident, three useful outcomes

An application starts timing out after a deployment. In Chat, ask for hypotheses from sanitized symptoms. In Work, turn an approved incident timeline into an incident brief. In Codex, investigate retry behavior in the repository and add a regression test.

Give each task an explicit finish line: a hypothesis table, a sourced brief, or a verified patch. Keep human review at the point where the result becomes an operational decision.

## The training loop

1. **Frame:** describe the outcome and what counts as success.
2. **Ground:** supply the relevant files, facts, and constraints.
3. **Delegate:** choose the tool access appropriate for the task.
4. **Verify:** inspect evidence against the success criteria.

**Discussion:** Which part of your last engineering task required judgment, and which part could have been delegated with a checkable result?
