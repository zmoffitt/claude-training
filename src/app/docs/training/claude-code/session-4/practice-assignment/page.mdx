# Practice Assignment

Due before Session 5 — one week from today.

1. **Fork the demo's shape.** Pick one of *your* systems — a repo, a tenant, an env — and write a subagent + orchestrator skill that audits it read-only. The two files in `.claude/agents/` and `.claude/skills/` from the [marketplace audit demo](/docs/training/claude-code/session-4/live-demo-marketplace-auditor) are your starting template.
2. **Apply at least three of the five guardrail layers** — the deny rules, the "stop and ask" checkpoint, the definition-of-done file. Document what each one looks like in your context.
3. **Run it.** Note **what worked, what broke, what you would change** — this is your retrospective.
4. **Write a one-paragraph proposal:** what would it take to move your audit from Level 2 to Level 3 next quarter? What guardrail would have to harden first?
5. **Write up the workflow** in three minutes' worth of detail: trigger, steps, autonomy level, guardrails, results — enough for a teammate to adopt it.

<Callout variant="info" title="Before the capstone" align="left">
  Review your practice logs from all four weeks and draft three proposed team standards for Claude usage — what should every `CLAUDE.md` in your org include? Then agree on team-wide conventions, build the playbook and assign owners, and look at custom MCP servers for systems with no CLI or connector. Pick a workflow you'd want the whole team to adopt — not the flashiest, the most useful.
</Callout>
